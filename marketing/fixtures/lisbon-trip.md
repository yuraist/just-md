# Lisbon, four days in October

Flights are booked, the apartment is in Graça, and the plan below is loose
on purpose. The only fixed item is the Sintra day, because the train fills up.

| Day | Morning | Afternoon | Evening |
|-----|---------|-----------|---------|
| Thu | Land, drop bags, coffee at the miradouro | Alfama on foot, no map | Dinner near the apartment |
| Fri | Tram 28 early, before the queue | Belém: the tower, the monastery, the pastéis | Fado, if we still have legs |
| Sat | Train to [Sintra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sintra) | Pena Palace, then the Moorish castle | Back by 20:00, tired |
| Sun | Time Out Market | LX Factory, the bookshop | Flight at 21:40 |

## Getting around

- Buy a **Viva Viagem** card at the airport metro station and load it with zapping credit.
- The 28 tram is worth it once, at 8 in the morning. After that it is a queue on rails.
- Sintra: the 09:11 train from Rossio, then the 434 bus loop. Buy the palace ticket online.

## Packing

- [x] Passports, printed apartment address
- [x] Adapter (Portugal uses type F)
- [ ] Walking shoes that have already been broken in
- [ ] A light rain jacket, October is unpredictable
- [ ] The paperback for the train

## Budget

Roughly 120 € a day for two, not counting the apartment. Pastéis de nata are
1.30 € each at the bakery in Belém and about 2 € everywhere else; we will need
a lot of them.
